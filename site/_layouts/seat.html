The line is this seat's WAR "replacement level" — the Democratic two-party lean a generic candidate of either party would be expected to get, from the statewide baseline race alone (WAR v1's baseline). Points are what each actual candidate got that year; above the line is a Democratic overperformance, below is a Republican one. What drives replacement level: {{ latest_contested.year | default: "" }}

{% if latest_contested %}

WAR v2 is a regression, fit across every contested race in this site's backfill: actual share ~ a baseline intercept + district lean + that year's statewide tide + incumbency (1st/2nd/3rd-or-later term). This breaks each candidate's actual share into exactly those pieces, plus whatever's left over — WAR v2 itself, the part attributable to the candidate. See the methodology page for the fitted coefficients, their uncertainty, and what they're based on.
